Common questions

Where are my saves? On your phone. Open Saves at the top of the game to switch games or start a new one. Deleting the app deletes your saves.

Does the company keep running when I close the app? Yes, for a limited time that grows as you hire office staff and move into bigger offices. When every job is finished, payroll stops until you come back.

How do I replay the tutorial? Open Saves and tap Replay the tutorial.
