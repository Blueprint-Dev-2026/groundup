Ground Up privacy policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Ground Up: Construction Tycoon ("Ground Up", "the app") is a single-player game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. There are no accounts or logins.

Your game saves

Your progress is saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes your saves.

Analytics, ads and tracking

This version of the app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds features that collect data, such as cloud saves or in-app purchases, we will update this policy and the app's App Store privacy details before that version is released.
