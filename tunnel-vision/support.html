Need help with Tunnel Vision: Car Wash Tycoon? Most answers are below. If you don't find yours, email crodrig770@yahoo.com and we'll get back to you, usually within two business days.

How do I save my game?

You don't need to. The game saves automatically every few seconds and whenever you leave the app.

How do I start a new company or switch between companies?

Open the Goals tab and tap "Switch game or start a new one." You can keep up to three companies in separate game slots.

How do I restore my purchases on a new device?

Open the Goals tab, tap Open shop, then tap Restore purchases. Permanent items like Double Suds, 10x speed and the maintenance plan come back on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. Cash packs are used the moment you buy them, so they can't be restored.

Do my car washes earn money while the app is closed?

Yes. Your sites keep earning for up to 12 hours while you're away, or 24 hours with Longer Away Earnings. You never lose money while you're away.

I bought something but it didn't show up

Make sure you're connected to the internet, then use Restore purchases in the shop. If it still isn't there, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.

I deleted the app and my progress is gone

Progress is stored only on your device, so deleting the app deletes your saved companies. Purchases you made can still be restored.
